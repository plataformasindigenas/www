Frequently asked questions

How are copyrights handled for produced materials?

All materials receive a permanent DOI. Authorship includes speakers, teachers, researchers, and community leaders as agreed with the community.

Who can become a partner?

Indigenous schools, universities, education departments, NGOs, and institutions interested in ethically contributing to language revitalization.

Does technology replace traditional knowledge?

No. Technology is a tool to amplify and preserve traditional knowledge. All development is done in partnership with communities.

How does the project impact school education?

We provide pedagogical materials aligned with the national curriculum, adapted to local realities. Teachers receive specific training.
