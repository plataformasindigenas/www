Privacy

How we handle your personal data in compliance with LGPD.

Data collected

This site has no forms and collects no personal data. To talk to us, write to info@plataformasindigenas.org: your message stays only in our mailbox.

Purpose of use

Messages we receive are used exclusively to answer enquiries and contact requests. We do not share, sell, or use your data for any other purpose.

Cookies and tracking

This site uses no cookies, trackers, or analytics tools. It keeps on your device only the language you chose and a copy of the pages you have already opened, so that they work without internet. None of this is sent to anyone.

External services

Fonts and images come from the site itself: no page loads files from other services. Links to Instagram, LinkedIn and other sites lead to services with their own privacy policies.

Your rights (LGPD)

In compliance with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018), you have the right to:

  • request access to your personal data;
  • request correction of incomplete or outdated data;
  • request deletion of your personal data;
  • revoke consent for the use of your data.

Data deletion

To request deletion of your personal data, contact us at info@plataformasindigenas.org.

Updates to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Last updated: November 2026.
